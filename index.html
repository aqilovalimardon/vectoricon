How to Paste Editable Vectors in Adobe Illustrator

  1. Search in English: Type any keyword like teacher, student, doctor, home, or cart to see multiple icon variants.
  2. Choose Style (1. Outline or 2. Full): Switch between 1. Outline (stroke paths) and 2. Full (solid filled vector shapes) at the top or in the right inspector panel.
  3. Copy & Paste: Click Copy Vector (or press Ctrl + C), switch to Adobe Illustrator, and press Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on Mac).
  4. The icon drops directly onto your artboard as 100% editable vector paths and anchor points.
